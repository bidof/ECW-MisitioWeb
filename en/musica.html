Music · My soundtrack

Records that never get old.

What I listen to

I like old music because every recording seems to hold an era. Classic rock, blues and soul have a personality that still makes you want to come back to them years later.

This is a small selection to get started. It is not a definitive list: it is a doorway to records that have stayed with me and always reveal a new detail. The audio files are only short clips of each song and only one plays at a time.
