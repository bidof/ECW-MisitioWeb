Japan, between tradition and future.

Why Japan?

What attracts me most about Japan is the contrast between city life and tradition. In the same city you can find wide avenues, screens and crowded trains alongside temples, gardens and neighbourhoods that keep a completely different calm.

I am also interested in discovering a culture so different from the one I know in Europe: its customs, its food, the importance it gives to certain rituals and the way it organises everyday life. I would like to experience those differences with respect, beyond the images we see from afar.

I want to travel by train, visit Tokyo, Kyoto and Nara, and have time to observe how technology, history and traditions relate to each other in every place.

Japan and car culture

I am also passionate about Japanese cars. I am interested in their design, their mechanical precision and their connection with racing, customisation and popular culture.

I would like to discover that side of Japan up close: from classic sports cars to the workshops and meetings where engineering becomes a form of expression.
