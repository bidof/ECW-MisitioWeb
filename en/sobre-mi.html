The way I see things

I am a software engineer and I like turning ideas into useful projects. This blog is a collection of interests: trips that are still on the map, records that keep playing and questions I feel like exploring.

My journey began with the Software Engineering degree at the University of Oviedo, and today I keep learning in the Master in Web Engineering. I am interested in how a well-built page, fast, accessible and easy to maintain, can make a difference for the people who use it.

I have worked with very different technologies: web applications, video games with Unity and virtual reality experiences. I like switching fields because each one makes me approach problems in a different way. In the education section you can see my projects in more detail.

Outside work I have several hobbies that have been with me for years; you will find them all together below. If you want to know more, you can write to me from the contact page.
