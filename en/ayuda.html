Help

How can I help you?

Here is everything you need to move around the site, find what you are looking for and adapt it to the way you browse.

  • How to browse
  • How to search
  • Keyboard shortcuts
  • Accessibility
  • FAQ

How to browse

  • Main menu: it is at the top of every page and highlights the section you are in with a coloured background. On mobile it opens with the “Menu” button.
  • Breadcrumb: below the header you will see the path from the home page to the current page. Select “Home” to go back.
  • Site map: the site map shows every page and section in one place.
  • Footer: it includes the date of the last update, help links and my social profiles.

How to search

The search box in the header shows suggestions as you type. Press Enter to see every result on the search page.

  • You can search for any word that appears on the site; selecting a result takes you straight to the part of the page where it appears.
  • You can type several words, for example “unity games”.
  • If you make a typo, the search suggests the right word.
  • Matching words are highlighted in the results.

Keyboard shortcuts

The whole site can be used with the keyboard only.
Key Action
/ Jump to the search box from any page.
↑ ↓ Move through the search suggestions.
Enter Open the chosen suggestion or see every result.
Esc Close the suggestions or the mobile menu.
Tab Move to the next link or control. The first one is “Skip to content”.

Accessibility

  • Text has a contrast ratio of at least 7:1 with the background.
  • Every image, audio clip and video has alternative text or a description.
  • If your system asks for reduced motion, animations are turned off.
  • The ES link in the header opens the same page in Spanish.

Frequently asked questions

How can I contact you?

On the contact page you will find my email, my GitHub and a form that prepares the message for you.

Is the site available in other languages?

Yes, every page is available in Spanish and English. Use the “ES” or “EN” link in the header to switch language without leaving the page you are on.

I cannot hear any audio on the music page. What should I do?

Press the play button of each song and check your device volume. Only one clip plays at a time: when one starts, the previous one stops and the active song is marked with the ♪ symbol.

Can I use the site without JavaScript?

Yes. All the content, the menu and the links work without JavaScript. Only the search and form validation need it.

How often is the site updated?

Whenever there is something new. The date of the last update appears in the footer of every page and the details are in the change log.
